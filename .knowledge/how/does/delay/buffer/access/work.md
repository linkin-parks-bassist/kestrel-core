The documented delay_read fetches a cached sample. delay_mwrite writes a sample with a q8.8 modulation argument, advances the position and changes the fractional delay; the controller prefetches the next read. The assembler delay_write is the zero-modulation special case. Verify current RTL before relying on hardware capacity. Source: README.md
