filter_master dispatches to three units. include/build.vh enables ENABLE_POLYNOMIAL and ENABLE_SVF by default, excluding ENABLE_FILTER. KESTREL_CUSTOM_BUILD permits explicit combinations. src/filter.v contains the optional ENABLE_FILTER history engine and ENABLE_SVF Chamberlin unit. src/polynomial.v contains polynomial_unit, extracted from the fixed normal engine with history RAM, feedback and cascaded-filter machinery removed. Polynomial instructions route only to that unit. All enabled coefficient-based units receive the existing allocation/write/update/commit strobes; handles follow allocation order, and polynomial storage reserves the feed-forward coefficient count. Polynomial coefficient writes beyond that count are ignored. Polynomial retains power-sum arithmetic, double coefficient banks, normalization, saturation and direct constants. include/defs.vh selects 18-bit coefficients and 16-bit audio/data. src/svf.v remains empty.

Disabled resource requests are acknowledged as invalid and return zero so an unsupported result instruction does not hold up retirement. This is not firmware capability enforcement: automatic rejection or lowering is still planned. The master selects the active unit's stuck signal rather than treating an idle optional unit as a failure. The build-register responder reports the actual compiled capabilities; the controller SPI owner specifies its address/read contract.

SVF takes audio A, cutoff B and damping C. David selected nonnegative signed Q15 cutoff, accepting one fewer fractional bit to reuse existing channels. The unit clamps negative B words to zero and otherwise zero-extends them; it sign-extends C. Cutoff products divide by 2^15; damping products divide by 2^(15-shift). Thus f=max(signed16(B),0)/32768 and d=signed16(C)*2^shift/32768. Extra arithmetic bits provide state headroom, not coefficient gain. The Interface descriptor fixes B's scale, permits independent signed damping formats, derives the field from C and applies max(expression,0) before expression-register encoding; generic signed-Q15 saturation supplies the upper endpoint. Initial and live register conversion share resolved metadata. Channels supply encoded DSP words.

The Interface fixed-point owner governs the implemented descriptor-driven joint resolver and instruction-level send expressions. Scratchpad MEM evaluation interprets signed16 as Q15 without discovering a differently scaled producer's intended value. The old range-dependent cutoff scale and coefficient left shifts have been replaced. Compiler and RTL changes must be deployed together for this contract. The exact identity of David's forgotten historical range-guarantee failure remains unestablished.

As a numerical illustration, a 20-Hz lowpass at 44.1 kHz, Q=1/sqrt(2), has b0≈2.026e-6 and DC denominator≈8.103e-6; signed18 rounding with 16 fractional bits makes both zero. This does not establish the sole cause of the reported sound.

Individual low/band/high outputs form three two-parameter families; a normalized general real biquad has five coefficient degrees of freedom. Freely weighted mixing spans the full strictly stable real biquad family in exact arithmetic for the ideal source-order recurrence:
L[n]=L[n-1]+fB[n-1], H[n]=x[n]-L[n]-dB[n-1], B[n]=B[n-1]+fH[n].
With q=z^-1, D=1+(f²+fd-2)q+(1-fd)q²; low/band/high numerators are f²q, f(1-q), (1-q)², a quadratic basis for f≠0.

For (b0+b1q+b2q²)/(1+a1q+a2q²), choose f=sqrt(1+a1+a2), d=(1-a2)/f, mH=b2, mB=(b0-b2)/f, mL=(b0+b1+b2)/f². Strict real Schur stability gives positive radicand/damping, including overdamped real poles. This assumes unrestricted positive f,d and signed weights; it excludes unstable denominators requiring f²<0 and the singular z=1 boundary. A 10,000-case stable-pole round-trip check had maximum coefficient error below 3e-15; algebra supplies the proof.

Finite RTL ranges, truncation and separate output saturation limit that claim: mixing clipped outputs loses information, and selected cutoff bounds do not realize unrestricted f. Parameters/weights can grow near stability boundaries. Arbitrarily time-varying stability is not proved. Simper's trapezoidal derivation at https://cytomic.com/files/dsp/SvfLinearTrapOptimised2.pdf concerns another realization whose costs need evaluation.
